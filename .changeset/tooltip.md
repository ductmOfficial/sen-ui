Add `Tooltip`, built on React Aria Components. Wrap one focusable trigger, such as `Button`: `<Tooltip content="Delete the file"><Button aria-label="Delete">×</Button></Tooltip>`. It opens on hover after `delay` (700 ms by default) and at once on keyboard focus, closes on Escape, on press and when focus or the pointer leaves, and describes the trigger through `aria-describedby`.
